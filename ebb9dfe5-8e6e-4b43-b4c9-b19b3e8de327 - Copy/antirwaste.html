About Our Company

Building a Sustainable Future Since 1999

Anti Waste Mokopane was established in 1999 by owners Mark and Marie Scholtz. Since then, we have been proudly trading in Mokopane and surrounding areas, providing comprehensive waste management and recycling services that make a real difference in our community.

  • 140+ Daily Customers Servicing businesses and residents 5 days a week across Mokopane region
  • Major Retail Partnerships Working with Game, Shoprite, Edgars, CNA, Clicks, Pep and more chain stores
  • 80+ Direct Employees Creating meaningful employment opportunities in our local community
  • Buy Back Centres Operating centres to assist and support community recycling initiatives
  • Emergency Services Providing refuse removal during critical situations such as strikes

Our Comprehensive Services

We provide end-to-end waste management solutions tailored to meet the needs of businesses, communities, and individuals committed to environmental sustainability.

Materials We Accept

Comprehensive Recycling Program

At Anti-Waste, we accept a wide variety of recyclable materials. Our program helps reduce environmental impact while supporting our community through sustainable practices.

Select a category below to learn more about what materials we accept, or browse all categories to discover how you can contribute to environmental sustainability.

Paper Category

K4 - Cardboards & Boxes

We recycle all types of cardboard packaging materials, from shipping boxes to product packaging. Clean, dry cardboard is essential for quality recycling. This includes corrugated boxes, paperboard packaging, and carton materials.

  • Cardboards (all types)
  • Boxes (shipping & product)
  • Packaging cartons
  • Corrugated materials

Mixed Category

Common Mix - Mixed Paper Products

Mixed paper products from household and office use are recycled into new paper products. This category accepts various colored papers and paper-based food containers that don't fit into pure white paper categories.

  • Coloured Office Paper (with or without print)
  • Cereal Boxes
  • Soup Containers
  • Mixed paper products

Paper Category

HL 1 - White & Office Paper

High-quality paper recycling for office and printing waste. We accept both printed and unprinted white paper products. This premium category ensures the highest quality recycling output for new paper production.

  • White Paper (all grades)
  • Office Paper (with or without print)
  • Printing paper waste
  • Document paper

Plastic Category

LDPE - Soft Plastic

Low-Density Polyethylene plastics including plastic bags, wrapping films, and other flexible plastic packaging. These soft plastics are recycled into new bags, construction materials, and various plastic products.

  • Soft Plastic (all types)
  • Plastic shopping bags
  • Wrapping films
  • Flexible packaging
Click to learn more →
Plastic Category

HDPE - Hard Plastic

High-Density Polyethylene rigid plastics used in containers, bottles, and durable plastic items. These sturdy plastics are highly recyclable and transformed into new containers, pipes, and outdoor furniture.

  • Hard Plastic items
  • Milk Bottles & Jugs
  • Plastic Crates
  • Durable containers
Click to learn more →
Plastic Category

LLDE - Wrapping Plastic

Linear Low-Density Polyethylene used primarily for stretch wrap and protective packaging materials. Commonly found in industrial and commercial settings for securing pallets and protecting goods.

  • Wrapping Plastic film
  • Stretch wrap
  • Protective packaging
  • Pallet wrap
Click to learn more →
Plastic Category

PET - Beverage Containers

Polyethylene Terephthalate used extensively for beverage bottles and food containers. One of the most commonly recycled plastics, PET can be transformed into new bottles, polyester fiber, and many other products.

  • Beverage Bottles (all types)
  • Water Bottles
  • Soda Bottles
  • Food Containers
Click to learn more →
Plastic Category

PP - Polypropylene Products

Polypropylene durable plastics used in containers, caps, and furniture items. Known for its high heat resistance and strength, PP is recycled into automotive parts, industrial fibers, and new consumer products.

  • Hard Buckets & Pails
  • Bottle Caps & Closures
  • Plastic Garden Chairs
  • Kitchenware items
